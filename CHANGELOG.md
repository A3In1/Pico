## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-28

First public release.

### Added
- Browse an Android phone's storage from your Mac over USB, with Quick Access shortcuts (DCIM, Downloads, Pictures, Music, Documents, Movies) and SD card support.
- Download and upload files and folders, with progress, cancel and cancel-all.
- Drag and drop between Pico and Finder in both directions.
- Conflict handling: Ask, Keep Both, Replace or Skip.
- Wi-Fi connection: discovery, pairing with a code, and "Go wireless" from a USB connection.
- Multiple connected phones, with a device switcher and storage usage.
- Quick preview of images, videos and PDFs in the macOS viewer.
- Search, sorting, folder sizes and a virtualised list for very large folders.
- Light, dark and system themes, keyboard shortcuts and a quit reminder while transfers are running.
- **Pico → Check for Updates…** in the menu bar, plus an automatic check at launch.
- Bundled `adb` (Android Platform-Tools 37.0.0), so no separate install is needed.
