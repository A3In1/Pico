GETTING STARTED
01

Connect

Open Pico on your Mac and connect your Android phone with a USB cable, or pair it over Wi-Fi.

02

Browse

Browse internal storage or use a shortcut to find the folder you need.

03

Transfer

Choose the files you want and start the transfer. Drag and drop works too.

First launch. Pico isn't notarized yet, so macOS may block it the first time. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Pico.app. On your phone, enable USB debugging (Settings → Developer options).
